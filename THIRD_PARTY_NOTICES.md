## Data

- **Lensfun lens database** (built into the program): the 55 XML files of Lensfun 0.3.4.0 as shipped with
  lensfunpy 1.18.0, unmodified, in `crates/grainy-lens/data/lensfun/`. Copyright Lensfun contributors and the
  calibration contributors named in the XML files. Source: <https://lensfun.github.io/>,
  <https://github.com/lensfun/lensfun/tree/master/data/db>. Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0
  Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0), <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode>. The database stays under
  this licence, separately from Grainy's MIT code. Attribution does not imply endorsement.
- **CIE colorimetric data** (`crates/grainy-color/assets/`, `crates/grainy-film/assets/film/colorimetry.json`):
  CC BY-SA 4.0, see the NOTICE files there.
- **Film and paper data** (`crates/grainy-film/assets/film/`): numbers from the makers' published data sheets;
  film names are trademarks of their owners (see `NOTICE.txt` there).
- **Natural Earth** (`crates/grainy-app/assets/world.geojson`, the offline map): 1:110m countries, public domain,
  <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/>.
